## <i class="fa fa-wrench"></i> Widgets

Small self-contained calculators I built because I got tired of re-deriving the
same thing in a notebook. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is sent
anywhere.

<table class="table table-hover">
  <tr>
    <td style="white-space: nowrap;"><a href="/widgets/ring-filter/">Two-ring filter</a></td>
    <td>Drop and through port spectra for a series-coupled double microring
    add-drop filter, from the exact transfer-matrix solution.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td style="white-space: nowrap;"><a href="/widgets/ring-modulator/">Ring modulator OMA</a></td>
    <td>Optical modulation amplitude, extinction ratio and insertion loss as a
    function of bias detuning and drive swing. The best bias for OMA is not the
    best bias for ER.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td style="white-space: nowrap;"><a href="/widgets/vernier-filter/">Vernier two-ring filter</a></td>
    <td>Two unequal rings in series. Composite free spectral range and side mode
    suppression as you detune the radii apart.</td>
  </tr>
</table>
